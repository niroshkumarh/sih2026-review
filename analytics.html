How to plan for it

Where the volume is

AI / ML & Computer Vision touches 121 of 226 statements (54%) — it's the single highest-leverage skill to build across your team, even for statements not officially tagged "AI" by SIH. Mobile/Web + Cloud/Data show up almost everywhere as delivery/backend layers — treat those as baseline team capability, not a differentiator.

Where you can stand out

KCT already has real hardware/embedded experience (propulsion + embedded control work on other campus teams). IoT & Embedded Hardware (51) and Robotics, Drones & Automation (39) are smaller buckets nationally because most student teams default to pure software — fewer competing teams will have working hardware, so a working prototype here stands out more per unit of effort than another AI dashboard.

Suggested team split

Team A — Hardware-capable: target IoT/Embedded or Robotics buckets, lower competition, plays to existing campus strength.
Team B — AI/software-heavy: target AI/ML crossed with Disaster & Environment or Healthcare & MedTech — the largest buckets, strong judge appeal, and mature public datasets (weather/satellite APIs, health records) make them buildable in hackathon time.
Team C — First-time SIH teams: pick from the 50 "Starter" complexity statements to build hackathon experience without getting buried in scope — concentrated in General Software/Process Tooling, Healthcare, and Space buckets.

Reality check

179 of 226 statements (79%) span multiple domains — most PS need a cross-functional team (e.g. ML + GIS, or hardware + cloud), not a single-skill group. Pick statements that match a team's *combined* skill set, not one person's specialty.
